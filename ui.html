About

Reforger Mod Browser is a local, Steam-style front end for the Arma Reforger workshop. It keeps its own copy of the workshop list on your PC (every public mod), refreshed from the Workshop site while it is open, so searching and sorting are instant and the sort orders actually work: rating weighted by votes and downloads, trending by downloads gained, filters that combine.

Playlists are your mod sets. One is marked in game; it is written to . The companion in-game mod reads it, downloads what is missing and enables exactly those mods.

Your Items reads the game's addons folder to show what is installed on this PC.

Data: reforger.armaplatform.com. Ratings, subscribers and downloads are the Workshop's numbers. Trending needs a few days of daily snapshots before it means anything.
